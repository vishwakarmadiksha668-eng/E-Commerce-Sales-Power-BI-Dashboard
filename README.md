# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

An interactive **E-Commerce Sales Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, profitability, product performance, platforms, monthly trends, and country-level sales.

The dashboard transforms raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations and KPIs.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Preview

![E-Commerce Sales Dashboard](E-Commerce%20Dashboard.png)

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze e-commerce sales data and create an interactive dashboard that helps understand:

- Overall sales and profitability
- Monthly sales and profit trends
- Sales performance by product category
- Sales performance across different platforms
- Top-performing products
- Country-level sales performance
- Order volume and units sold

The dashboard is designed to provide a clear and interactive view of business performance and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 💼 Business Questions

This dashboard answers key business questions such as:

1. What are the total sales and gross profit?
2. What is the overall profit margin?
3. How are sales and profit changing month over month?
4. Which product categories generate the highest sales?
5. Which e-commerce platform performs best?
6. Which products are the top contributors to sales?
7. Which countries generate the highest revenue?
8. How many orders and units have been sold?
9. How is sales performance distributed globally?

## 📌 Dashboard Features

### 1. Global Sales Distribution 🌎

A geographical visualization showing the distribution of sales across different regions and countries.

This helps identify the global reach of the e-commerce business.

### 2. Sales by Category 🛍️

A donut chart showing sales distribution across product categories, including:

- Electronics
- Home & Accessories
- Sports
- Fashion
- Beauty
- Books

This helps identify the categories contributing the most revenue.

### 3. Sales & Profit by Platform 💻

A comparison of revenue and gross profit across major e-commerce platforms:

- Shopify
- eBay
- Amazon

This visualization helps compare platform-level performance.

### 4. Monthly Sales & Profit Trend 📅

A monthly trend analysis comparing:

- Total Revenue
- Gross Profit

This helps identify changes in sales and profitability over time.

### 5. Top 10 Products by Sales 🏆

A ranking of the highest-selling products.

Top-performing products include:

- Headphones
- Laptop
- Smartphone
- Tablet
- Smartwatch
- Desk Lamp
- Coffee Machine
- Blender

This helps identify products that contribute significantly to overall sales.

### 6. Country Performance 🌍

A country-level table displaying:

- Country
- Orders
- Revenue

This provides a detailed view of geographical sales performance.

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

- Total sales reached approximately **1.79M**.
- The business generated approximately **588.35K in gross profit**.
- The overall profit margin is approximately **32.84%**.
- A total of **6,490 units** were sold across **1,000 orders**.
- **Electronics** is the strongest sales category in the dashboard.
- **Headphones** are the highest-selling product among the displayed top products.
- **Shopify** shows the highest revenue among the three displayed platforms.
- Sales performance varies across countries, with India, Singapore, France, and Canada appearing among the leading countries in the displayed analysis.
- Monthly analysis shows variations in both revenue and gross profit, helping identify stronger and weaker sales periods.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

### Step 1 — Data Collection

Collected the e-commerce sales dataset containing information related to products, sales, orders, platforms, countries, and profitability.

### Step 2 — Data Cleaning

Used **Power Query** to prepare the dataset by:

- Removing unnecessary data
- Handling data inconsistencies
- Checking data types
- Preparing fields for analysis
- Structuring the dataset for reporting

### Step 3 — Data Transformation

Transformed the raw dataset into an analysis-ready format using Power Query.

### Step 4 — Data Modeling

Created relationships and organized the data model to support dashboard analysis.

### Step 5 — DAX Calculations

Created measures for important business metrics such as:

- Total Sales
- Gross Profit
- Profit Margin
- Units Sold
- Total Orders

### Step 6 — Dashboard Development

Designed an interactive Power BI dashboard using:

- KPI Cards
- Donut Chart
- Column Chart
- Bar Chart
- Map Visualization
- Trend Analysis
- Table/Matrix

### Step 7 — Business Analysis

Analyzed the dashboard to identify trends, high-performing products, categories, platforms, and countries.
